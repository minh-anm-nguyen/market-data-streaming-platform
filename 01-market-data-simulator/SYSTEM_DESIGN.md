and two documents disagreeing about an invariant is worse than one document in a
slightly inconvenient place.

## Where the implementation departs from the document

Recorded in full in [`README.md`](README.md); in short:

* **ADR-010** replaces `random.Random` with SplitMix64, bringing section 4.2's
  "future" cross-runtime reproducibility forward because Project 03 needs it.
* **ADR-011** makes `Event` a `NamedTuple` rather than a frozen dataclass, for a
  measured 2.5x on event construction with the same immutability.
* **Python 3.14.4** was used rather than the specified 3.12. The output is
  version-independent by construction, but that has not been measured on 3.12.
* **L3 (50k/s) and L4 (120k/s) are not achieved.** Reported as such.
