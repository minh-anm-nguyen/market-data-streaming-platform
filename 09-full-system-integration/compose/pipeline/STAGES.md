## Notes

* **`p02-source-manifest`** -- P02 writes it directly now; the P09 shim that stood here is gone
* **`p04-publish`** -- runs with upstream=p03_handoff, the real join (P09-M0-05)
* **`p05-seed-offsets`** -- P05 refuses auto_offset_reset=earliest by design (its I-11)
* **`seal-kafka-cut`** -- P09 seals it: p05_ingest snapshot reads assignment() too early and records no committed frontier
* **`p06-build`** -- build-from-raw reads P05's table; plain `build` synthesises its own corpus
* **`p07-verify-source`** -- the full scenario runner needs a lab profile; this is the part that reads this deployment
* **`p07-scan-live-cut`** -- same scan_cut classifier as the model profile; only the records are real
* **`p07-read-live-rows`** -- physical rows and logical identities reported separately
